**Body (128 words):**

Every artificial intelligence system on the planet has the same bottleneck. A person.

Somebody has an idea. Writes the code. Runs the training. Checks the output. That loop runs on human time.

Recursive self-improvement is what happens when you pull the person out of it. The system improves itself, in a loop, each version building the next one faster than the last.

It is not here yet. Every model still hits a wall and stops short.

Here is the part that matters. Ask the engineers building these systems why the model made a specific decision and you get the same answer a brain surgeon gives when you ask why someone reached for the cookie. They do not know.

We are scaling something we cannot fully read.
