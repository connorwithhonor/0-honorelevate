# Fact Check: Recursive Self-Improvement Transcript

Three claims in the spoken transcript did not make it into any written asset, on purpose.
Fix them on the video side before this gets multiplied across seven platforms.

## 1. Grok and Cursor (needs correction)

**Transcript says:** "Rock was not really heard of until Elon Musk bought a company called Cursor."

**Reality:** Grok is built by xAI, which Musk founded in July 2023. xAI merged with X Corp in
March 2025. Cursor is a separate product built by Anysphere and was not acquired by Musk.

**Why it matters:** This is the one claim in the video that a commenter will screenshot.
It also sits right next to Colossus, which is accurate, so the correction is cheap and the
credibility hit if left alone is not.

**Recommended fix:** Pinned comment plus on-screen text at the timestamp.
Suggested pinned comment:
"Correction on the Grok segment. I said Musk bought Cursor. Wrong. Grok comes from xAI, which
Musk founded in 2023, and xAI merged with X Corp in March 2025. Cursor is a separate company.
The Colossus point stands. Thanks for keeping me straight."

## 2. The Fermi Paradox attribution (needs a clarifier)

**Transcript says:** Fermi joked at a Los Alamos lunch, and the conclusion was that civilizations
build superintelligence and get wiped out.

**Reality:** The lunch and the question are right. Fermi asked it at Los Alamos in 1950.
The "they built something and did not survive it" answer is the Great Filter, formulated by
Robin Hanson in 1996. Fermi posed the question. He did not supply that answer.

**Recommended fix:** One line in the description or a on-screen card. The written assets in
this package already phrase it as "one proposed answer" instead of attributing it to Fermi.

## 3. "Javansberg" (garbled name)

**Transcript says:** "Now, Javansberg. The Fermi Paradox is, everything's going to be in a
utopia."

**Reality:** No such name. Best guess from context is Nick Bostrom, whose work covers both
superintelligence risk and the abundance case. Whoever it was, the audio is unusable.

**Recommended fix:** Either name the person correctly in a pinned comment or drop the reference.
No written asset in this package uses it.
