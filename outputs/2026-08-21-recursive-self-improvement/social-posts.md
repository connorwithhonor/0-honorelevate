Every AI system on the planet still needs one thing to get better. A human. 🧠

It is not here yet. If you use these tools all day you already know why. Give a model a real task and full authority and it still stops short. It hits a wall. There is a gremlin in the machine.

We are scaling something we cannot fully read.

Whoever crosses that line first has an obvious first move, and nobody says it out loud. Make sure nobody else crosses it. That is not a conspiracy theory. That is just what a race looks like when there is only one trophy.

## LINKEDIN

Every AI system in production today has a human bottleneck. That is the most underrated fact in this entire industry.

Somebody has an idea. Somebody writes the code. Somebody runs the training and evaluates the output. That loop runs on human time. Days. Weeks. Review cycles. Board approvals.

Recursive self-improvement removes that bottleneck. The system improves its own code, its own training, its own architecture, without a human approval step in the middle. Each version builds the next one faster than the last.

It has not happened yet. If you deploy these tools in real businesses you already know why. Give a model a genuine task with full authority and it still stops short. It hits a wall. Something breaks.

Two things every business owner should sit with.

First, interpretability. The people building these systems cannot reliably explain why a model made a specific decision. That is not marketing caution. That is the actual state of the field. We are scaling capability faster than we are scaling understanding.

Second, access. We have already watched capable models get handed to the public and then pulled back. If your operations depend on a model, you are depending on a switch somebody else controls. Build for that. Document your workflows. Keep your prompts and your process portable.

The practical takeaway is not doom and it is not utopia. It is urgency of a boring kind. The tools available right now, at ten to twenty dollars a month, will do more for a small business than most software costing a hundred times that. Deploy them while they are cheap and public.

The wealthy are using this to get wealthier. The mission is handing the same tools to everyone else.
